# PUC Interchange filings

- Source id: `tx_puc_interchange`
- Site: https://interchange.puc.texas.gov
- Checked: November 19, 2026
- Result: **held as restricted until the commission answers.** Access is not barred. The commission's link policy asks for a permission request before content is copied, and our archive copies documents. **The owner has sent that request, and said so on November 23, 2026. No answer yet.**

## What was verified

- **Robots rules.** `interchange.puc.texas.gov` has no robots file (404). `www.puc.texas.gov/robots.txt` allows everything for ordinary agents. No crawl delay on either.
- **Privacy policy.** It covers pages on both hosts. It says filings are public through the Interchange and may show up in search engines. It says traffic is monitored for attempts to upload, change or damage. It has nothing on robots, crawlers or scraping.
- **Link policy,** https://www.puc.texas.gov/agency/about/policies/linkpolicy/. It says all commission content is protected by copyright, and that site owners "should contact the PUCT to request permission to use or copy content". It also warns against placing a download burden on the commission's servers. Read twice.
- **What the site offers.** No login and no captcha on the home or search pages. Filing search is a plain form with control number, utility type, filing party, document type, dates and description. A daily filings search takes a date range. An example PDF answered a header-only request with no login.
- **Official alerts.** The commission runs a Filing Notification System that needs registration, and a page of control numbers issued in the last 15 days.

## Why this is held

The link policy is written for sites that link to the commission. It is a request, not a ban on access. Whether it reaches filings that utilities and other parties put on the public docket is not stated. We store a copy of each document so every alert traces to the exact record, which is the kind of copying the policy asks about. One email to the commission would settle it.

## Options for the owner

1. Ask the commission whether a nonprofit may archive public filings from the Interchange with attribution. Recommended.
2. Decide that public docket filings are outside that policy and mark the source allowed.
3. Register a reviewer for the Filing Notification System and treat this feed as manual.
