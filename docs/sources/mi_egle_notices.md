# EGLE notices and permit records in the MiEnviro Portal

- Source id: `mi_egle_notices`
- Pages: https://www.michigan.gov/egle/maps-data/mienviroportal and the portal at `mienviro.michigan.gov`
- Checked: November 19, 2026
- Result: **restricted. The feed stays manual.** The portal is on Michigan.gov. See [mi_state_of_michigan_terms.md](mi_state_of_michigan_terms.md) for the terms.

## What was verified

From search results for EGLE's own pages, not from a request to the portal:

- No account is needed to search for public notices and hearings or to comment on them. The portal has a "Public Notice Search".
- An account is needed to sign up for alerts on new public notice postings.
- The portal also has a Site Map Explorer that lists documents EGLE has published for a site.

## Contact

From EGLE's MiEnviro Portal contact page:

- Air: EGLE-Air-MiEnviro@Michigan.gov
- Water: EGLE-WRD-MiEnviro@Michigan.gov
- General questions: Environmental Assistance Center, EGLE-Assist@Michigan.gov, 800-662-9278

**Status:** a request for a written agreement is drafted and not sent. The feed stays manual until the State answers in writing.

## What a person can do by hand

- Run the public notice search once a week. No permission is needed.
- Create an account and sign up for public notice alerts. Creating the account is the owner's step. The alerts arrive by email and would feed a manual entry form, not a collector.
